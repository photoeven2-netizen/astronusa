# AstroGuide visual direction

## Design plan

- **Color:** Ink navy `#0B1020`, deep plum `#171326`, parchment `#F3EAD8`,
  antique gold `#D8B56D`, and quiet sea glass `#8CB9B2`.
- **Type:** Android's Noto Serif (`serif`) for chart titles and moments of
  reflection; the system sans family for form labels and reading text.
- **Layout:** Left-aligned, single-column reading flow, with comfortable
  margins and a bottom navigation bar. Onboarding leads with a short invitation
  and a calm sequence of birth details; the completed profile opens on a
  compact star-map motif, not a generic gradient hero.

- **Principles:** The birth chart is the memorable object: a restrained
  celestial dial with three legible sign placements. Gold indicates the
  current focus; sea glass marks guidance. Inputs are plain-language and
  privacy-aware. The app never decorates missing birth data as certainty.

## Brief review and revision

The approved brief calls for a dramatic, premium, mystical dark palette with
gold, so that palette stays. A generic dark card stack with neon glow would
make the subject feel like a template; instead, color is reserved for the
chart dial and reading hierarchy. The wireframe keeps onboarding practical
and left-aligned, rather than forcing centered, mystical copy into a form.
Serif display type is limited to moments of reflection so everyday input
remains easy to scan.
